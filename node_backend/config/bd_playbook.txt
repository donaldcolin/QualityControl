KALVIUM BD COACHING PLAYBOOK — 1:1 Counselling Booking Calls

=== CALL STRUCTURE (9 Stages) ===
1. WARM OPENING: Greet, identify student/parent, state source (school outreach or website/social). Ask: class, stream (PCMB/PCMC), board, location. Ask if they've heard of Kalvium.
2. PROBING: Understand their college search status. Plans post-12th? Shortlisting? Preparing for JEE/state exams?
3. UNDERSTAND PRIORITIES: Ask what matters most — academics, placements, campus, fees, location? Who decides — student, parents, or both?
4. INTRODUCE KALVIUM: 4-year on-campus B.Tech CSE, 9 partner universities (all UGC+AICTE approved). Key difference: combines academics + practical learning + real projects + industry exposure + career prep across all 4 years.
5. WHY EARLY: KNET (Kalvium entrance exam) is in December. Connecting early so families have time to understand, compare options, and plan without rushing.
6. PITCH THE 1:1 SESSION: Not a generic webinar. Personalized session where advisor understands the student's background, interests, and expectations. Parents can discuss fees, degree, placements. Full KNET walkthrough included.
7. UNIVERSITY STRUCTURE: Student picks preferred state/location first. University list released later. Kalvium learning experience is consistent across all campuses. University provides campus + awards degree.
8. BOOK THE SESSION: Father is preferred, one parent is mandatory. Offer two time slots. Confirm on WhatsApp.
9. CLOSE: Summarize what the session will cover. Send details + reminder on WhatsApp.

=== WHAT TOP PERFORMERS DO ===
- They sound NATURAL, not scripted or desperate. They are not stressed about targets.
- They CONNECT with the lead first — understand the family's situation before pitching anything.
- They make parents CURIOUS about the current education market and curriculum gaps.
- They are patient, take their time explaining in the 1:1 meeting, and close the sale there — not on the booking call.
- They act like ADMISSION COUNSELORS who genuinely care about the student's future, NOT salesmen.

=== COMMON MISTAKES TO FLAG ===
- Over-pitching: Dumping Kalvium's entire value proposition without first understanding the student's situation.
- Being pushy/salesy: Pressuring parents instead of consulting them.
- Not listening: Talking through a parent's concerns instead of acknowledging them.
- Losing control: Letting the call go off-track without steering back to the booking.
- Not securing parent presence: Failing to get at least one parent (preferably father) committed to the 1:1 session.
- Monologuing: Long stretches of talking without checking for understanding or asking questions.

=== OBJECTION HANDLING (REAL RESPONSES) ===

"Fees are too high":
→ Acknowledge the concern. Explain curriculum + work integration value. Mention that Kalvium's university pool includes options with fees as low as 2L/year. Invite to the 1:1 for detailed fee discussion.

"Never heard of Kalvium":
→ Acknowledge as new-gen edtech. We've been operating for 5 years. Our first batch has already graduated (from LPU). Don't oversell — let credibility come through facts.

"Preparing for JEE":
→ Ask their current marks. If below 80, realistically they won't get IIT/NIT. State exam colleges are traditional/theory-based — won't help with real job readiness. Position Kalvium as the practical alternative.

"We'll think about it":
→ Don't push. Respect their timeline. But frame the 1:1 session as a no-commitment way to gather information so they CAN make a decision later.

"What about placements?":
→ Be HONEST. Never say 100% placement. Say: if the student puts in the same effort we invest in them, placements follow. Offer to share placement details in the 1:1 meeting.

"Why not VIT/SRM/Manipal?":
→ NEVER badmouth. Acknowledge their strengths (e.g., "SRM has great campus life"). Then contrast curriculum approach: those colleges focus on theory, Kalvium combines theory + practical + work integration.

"Is the degree UGC approved?":
→ Yes. Degree is awarded by the partner university (fully UGC/AICTE accredited). Kalvium provides the curriculum and education. All exams are university exams.

"Not sure about CSE":
→ Acknowledge. Then explain CSE offers the best career value — high pay, comfortable lifestyle, broad opportunities. Other branches (like civil) require field work with lower comfort.

"Child is in 11th, too early":
→ Agree. Say "You're right, it's early. We'll get back to you next year." Do NOT push 11th-std leads.

"We want a traditional college":
→ Kalvium IS an offline, on-campus college. Students go to university daily, participate in activities. The difference is the CURRICULUM is modern and work-integrated, not the campus experience.

=== RED LINES — NEVER SAY ===
1. NEVER badmouth any competitor by name.
2. NEVER say "We provide 100% placement."
3. NEVER say "Internship starts from 2nd year" (this is a former claim, not current).
4. NEVER quote universities Kalvium has NOT tied up with.
5. NEVER guarantee a specific salary or scholarship amount.
6. NEVER make the call sound like a hard sell — you are a counselor, not a salesman.

=== KALVIUM'S 3 UNMATCHED DIFFERENTIATORS ===
1. PEER QUALITY: Every student enters through KNET — no management quota, no random admissions. The classroom peer group is filtered.
2. TECHNICAL CURRICULUM + WORK INTEGRATION: Not just theory — students build real projects and gain industry exposure throughout 4 years.
3. INTERNSHIP OPPORTUNITIES: Structured internship pathways that traditional colleges don't offer.

=== TONE GUIDELINES ===
- Confident but never pushy. Consultative, never salesy.
- Caring — show genuine interest in the student's future.
- Patient while explaining — parents expect this.
- When a parent is angry: LISTEN FIRST. Be patient. Then redirect into understanding.
- Energy should feel warm and curious during probing, steady and confident during the pitch, and respectful (not desperate) during the close.
- The associate's #1 skill: Wait for the right moment when the parent is willing, then strike. Be smart, not aggressive.
